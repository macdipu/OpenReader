Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

OpenReader ("the app") is a fully offline document reader for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. This policy explains what happens (and, more importantly, what does not happen) to your data when you use it.

No data collection

OpenReader does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Specifically, the app:

  • Does not require account creation or sign-in
  • Does not upload your documents or any file content to any server
  • Does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Does not have a backend or cloud dependency — it works entirely offline

What stays on your device

To provide features like browsing, Search, History and Favorites, OpenReader keeps a local index of the documents it finds on your device's storage (file name, location, size, type and dates), plus your reading history, reading positions and favorites. This is stored in an on-device SQLite database and local app preferences. It never leaves your device and is not accessible to the app developer or any third party.

File and storage access

On Android, OpenReader asks for the "All files access" permission (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). It uses it only to find supported documents (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV and text files) anywhere on your device's storage and to open them, because your documents can be in any folder, not just Downloads or Documents. You can decline it and still open files shared from other apps, and you can revoke it at any time in Android's settings.

On Windows, macOS and Linux, OpenReader reads documents from the folders it scans and from files you open or drop onto it.

Files are read and rendered locally and are never sent anywhere. OpenReader changes a file only when you explicitly delete it from within the app, after confirming.

No network access

OpenReader makes no network calls. It contains no push-notification, crash-reporting or other online service.

Children's privacy

OpenReader does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be raised via GitHub Issues.
