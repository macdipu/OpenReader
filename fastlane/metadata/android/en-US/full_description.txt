OpenReader is your all-in-one document reader for Android. Open, read, search, and organize your PDF, Word, Excel, CSV, and text files - all in a single app.

FEATURES
- Read PDF, DOC/DOCX, XLS/XLSX, CSV, and TXT files
- On-device document scanner finds files already on your phone
- Recents and Favorites for quick access
- Search across your documents
- Share files, view file info, and open documents from other apps
- Clean, fast, distraction-free reading experience

No accounts, no setup - just install and start reading. Whether you're reviewing a PDF, checking a spreadsheet, or reading a Word document, OpenReader gives you one simple app for all your documents.
