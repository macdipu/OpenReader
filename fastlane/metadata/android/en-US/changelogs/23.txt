What's new in 1.0.23
• Select and copy text in PDF, Word, PowerPoint, text and Markdown files: long-press for Copy and Select all
• Scanned PDF pages: tap Extract text to read them on your device, then select and copy
• Files opened from another app load faster and keep their real name
• Closing such a file asks: keep it, save a copy or remove it (Settings > Privacy & data)
• Moved or deleted files leave Recents by themselves; Locate finds them again
