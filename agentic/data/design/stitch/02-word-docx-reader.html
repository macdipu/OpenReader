1. Executive Overview & Scope

The purpose of this framework is to establish an immutable, zero-trust cloud landing zone across multi-region deployments. Initial workload profiling has demonstrated that 72% of legacy transactional services can be converted to containerized microservices without refactoring core schema definitions.

flag

Critical Delivery Objective

Key Milestone: Complete Phase 1 cutover within Q3 with zero customer downtime and 100% deterministic latency compliance.

All data storage mechanisms prioritize physical locality and end-to-end cryptographic verifiability. Read replicates will be maintained across isolated nodes to prevent split-brain conditions during regional failovers.
